Welcome to Aura

Aura is an early test version of an app for depression, loneliness and difficult relationships. Thank you for trying it.

  • It's a prototype, not a replacement for a therapist, doctor or emergency help.
  • For adults, 18 and over.
  • Your journal stays on this device. Nothing is collected or stored by us.
  • One exception: when you tap "Reflect with Aura," that entry is sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to write the reflection. Aura doesn't save it, and Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. Don't tap it if you'd rather keep an entry fully offline.
  • Aura's AI can be wrong. It never diagnoses or suggests medicine.

If you're in danger right now, call 112. To talk to a trained counsellor in India, free and any time: Tele-MANAS, 14416 or 1-800-891-4416.
